## Accepted technical bounds and mandatory separate gates

- Explicit opt-in only, semantically safe bodyless GET/HEAD, at most one additional SDK transport invocation, exact approved failure allowlist, strict bounded exposed Retry-After and original total deadline. The app owns replay semantics and one-use-signature decisions. Auth/preparation occur once; no refresh/mutating replay.
- Exactly six sanitized terminal fields, with attemptCount 0–2 and one logical ID/event/duration/outcome. Foundation internal attempts/redirects are not SDK invocation counts; discarded wire-header identities cannot be reconstructed.
