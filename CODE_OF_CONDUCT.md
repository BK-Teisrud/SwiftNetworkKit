# Code of conduct

Be respectful, give specific technical feedback, and accept reasoned disagreement. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and disclosure of private information are not permitted.

Maintainer @BK-Teisrud enforces these rules proportionately through guidance, moderation, and, where necessary, participation restrictions. Request a private contact channel rather than publishing sensitive report details. The maintainer treats reports confidentially as far as practicable and considers conflicts of interest when personally involved. These are the project's own rules; no affiliation with an external standard is claimed.
